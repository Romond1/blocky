# Room 5 — The Fruit Lift

## Scope and visual reuse
Build Room 5 only. Reuse Room 4’s volcanic palette, fractured basalt floor, jagged cave shell and lava falls, pillars, atmospheric particles, purple hero plate surfaces/rims/conduits, engraved signage, light word blocks, and cyan matching/door circuits. Extract small shared render builders where Room 4 currently embeds reusable pieces; preserve Room 4’s appearance. Elevator machinery and fruit picture panels are new. No sentence construction, color-fruit combinations, speed test, strength test, or Room 6.

## Layout
Use a compact broad atrium approximately 28 units wide by 24 deep, with ceiling clearance around 15 units. Entrance is south; exit is downstairs on the north wall toward the west. Three distinct picture targets and nearby answer plinths occupy the north-west ground-level wall. A balcony at Y=5 spans the north-east portion, keeping three upstairs word blocks visible from the entrance. A roughly 5 by 5 lift beside the east edge connects ground level and balcony. Its lower purple control is beside the landing, outside the lift footprint, connected by a visible purple conduit. A matching upper recall control prevents trapping both heroes upstairs. The south/front cabin fence blocks jumping, placement and pickup through that edge; boarding uses the north/rear landing. Both controls operate the same lift.

Reuse the ravine as a short recessed volcanic channel beneath the balcony frontage, with the Room 4 lower-floor treatment and an exposed recovery staircase whose floor cutout matches its incline. It is safe environmental geometry, not an additional bridge challenge. Keep lava beyond walkable boundaries. Preserve clear walking paths between the lower landing, fruit wall, entrance and exit. Railings guard balcony edges while leaving a broad view of the upper blocks.

## Elevator and device architecture
Keep simulation separate from Three.js. A focused elevator module owns DOWN, MOVING_UP, UP, MOVING_DOWN states, endpoint height, passenger/cargo attachment, control occupancy history, and safety checks. Travel takes several seconds at a readable constant speed. Only an off-to-on occupancy transition at either control toggles an idle lift. Activations during travel are consumed and ignored: standing on a control cannot restart or reverse it when motion ends. Aggregate occupancy per plate prevents a second overlapping hero from producing a false new step.

Extend shared device data with activationMode HOLD or TOGGLE; existing Room 4 plates remain HOLD by default. Per the user’s follow-up, TOGGLE controls hold the inactive hero in place until the player selects and moves that hero off; this parking behavior is independent of the one-shot activation event. Distinguish cabin parking/riding from control occupancy: a switched-away hero standing safely inside the cabin stays aboard while awaiting a command and during travel, and stays safely parked until selected and moved off the cabin. The lift remains usable after all fruit matches are complete.

## Transport, geometry and recovery
Move grounded cabin passengers and resting light blocks by the exact platform height delta. Carried blocks follow their original hero. Capture fully supported blocks and gently align partially supported drops into available cabin space; support several blocks and either or both heroes. Do not duplicate or reassign ownership on Tab. Update block pickup, placement, collisions and autonomous navigation with vertical separation so heroes cannot interact through the balcony floor or collide with blocks on another level.

Use an enclosed shaft with accessible landing entrances and protected edges. Prevent walking underneath the raised platform; if a jumping/falling hero is beneath its descent path, pause safely until clearance exists. Prevent entering solid balcony slabs from below. Jumping releases passenger support and landing restores it. Unsafe or partially supported shaft drops are redirected into available supported space; if no space exists the hero keeps carrying the block. Companion navigation cannot cross an absent lift, walk off moving machinery, step onto toggle controls autonomously, or teleport between levels. Upper companions stay on their own floor until physically transported. Safeguards must not provide an automatic elevator operator or automatically reunite the team.

## Fruit vocabulary and matching
Five explicit LIGHT_BLOCK-sized objects: APPLE, BANANA, PEAR, ORANGE, GRAPE; all weightClass light and requiredStrength 1. APPLE and GRAPE begin downstairs; BANANA, PEAR and ORANGE begin upstairs. At least BANANA and PEAR therefore require vertical transport. Render a single readable word on each block using the existing text system; pictures belong only on wall targets.

Provide recognizable apple, banana and pear illustrations in existing stone-framed panels, with no English answer labels beside them. Each corresponding answer zone accepts a forgiving placement. Correct placement snaps cleanly, glows cyan and locks that match. Wrong blocks stay retrievable, give brief neutral feedback, and preserve other correct matches. Removing a correctly locked match through normal interaction is prevented consistently with existing solved sockets. Three small environmental indicators show progress. All three matches send cyan energy to the existing door; decoys are optional and may remain anywhere safe.

## Team exit and reset
Use existing two-hero exit ownership and physical exit checks, extended to require ground-level presence. Solving the fruit puzzle does not transport a hero from upstairs. Players must use the lift and Tab to reunite; no offscreen recovery teleport bypasses this. Reset restores both ground-level spawns, the lowered stationary lift, empty activation histories, original block positions/heights, unlocked empty answer zones, unsolved circuits, closed door, and cleared passenger/device/companion state, including resets during motion and partial completion.

## Verification and reporting

Inspect screenshots from entrance, upper balcony, moving lift, fruit wall and recovery stairs. Report changed files, layout, reuse, mechanics, validation and material limitations; stop after Room 5 for user testing.

## Design choices
Recommended: side lift with open balcony in a broad atrium, maximizing shared sightlines. A central lift obscures the fruit wall; a wraparound balcony creates unnecessary travel. An upper recall control is a deliberate safety addition to the lower teaching control so experimental use cannot strand the team.

Follow-up visibility: move all three upper words to the front balcony edge, open side railings, reduce the shared minimum camera pitch from .22 to .154, and mount UP/DOWN on the balcony fascia for visibility without obscuring the upper hero.
