# Room 10 audit and repair — 11 October 2026

This is a repair of the existing Dragon's Cave, not a replacement level. The supplied lava-arena picture is a visual reference; the audited implementation is the current Three.js room and deterministic Game/HeroTeam simulation.

## Reproduced faults and repairs

1. **The cooperation gate did not match its collision.** The visible bars ran tangentially across the circular path, while movement checked an angular strip running radially across it. The movement check also stopped at radius 27, allowing an airborne hero to pass around the inner end. The gate now shares an oriented box definition between rendering and physics, spans the walking ring radially, and has visible basalt shoulders extending into the lava and outer cave wall. Hero and carried-object collision use the same gate and shoulders. The open route remains usable after either purple plate raises the bars.
2. **The widened language gallery had invisible walkable areas.** The renderer supplied angles from 0 to 2π; physics used atan2, from −π to π. The special inner/outer radii for the upper-left gallery were therefore omitted visually. Radius lookup now normalizes angles, so the rendered width agrees with the physical court supporting the carousel, brake, words and picture panels.
3. **Visible central pillars had no physical collision.** Their dimensions are now shared by the view and Room 10 solids. Hero movement checks solid volumes while preserving the existing 0.18-unit step tolerance. The existing construction cliff also has a matching solid body. The revised elevator now provides the required gallery route; tower construction is optional.
4. **Falling heroes could award forward checkpoints.** Sector completion previously checked horizontal position before recovering fallen actors. An actor below the floor could therefore advance a lock and respawn beyond the challenge. Sector acknowledgements now require both heroes grounded on a valid surface. A fall returns them to their already earned checkpoint.
5. **Anti-crush logic could open the closed gate without a plate.** Adversarial review reproduced both heroes and the carried Heart walking the radius-33 ring from the real spawn, touching neither plate, without a fall, and earning lock 1. Angular proximity incorrectly raised the bars before contact. Anti-crush now checks actual oriented gate-footprint overlap and can only retain an existing opening. Approaching a closed gate cannot raise it; travelers physically underneath an already open gate remain protected.
6. **The second obstacle expired even while its pad was occupied.** At the user's follow-up request, the 5.8-second one-shot timer was replaced with continuous grounded hero occupancy. Either hero holds the pad at (28, 13); switching leaves that hero in place and all six bridge slabs remain raised for the other hero to jump across. Releasing the pad lowers them; returning raises them again. Rendering and physical support share `slabsActive`, and the room objective explains the cooperation step.
7. **Wall-side ground was unsupported and the court floor flickered.** Shared convex floor patches now supply both rendered triangles and collision heights. The ring extends beneath the cave walls, which now have matching collision. The lower construction court is twelve units wide instead of nine, with the nearby wall moved outward. Court, dock and gallery tops no longer overlap the ring mesh, and docked machinery surfaces are separated from the floor. The required bridge channel and return-route break remain gaps, with visible lava beneath them. The user explicitly requested keeping the bridge required.
8. **The carousel brake was hidden behind the apple puzzle.** The HOLD pad is now at (-19, -23), in the clear approach in front of the language panels. A hero can walk to it, switch controls, and keep the carousel stopped while the other retrieves words.
9. **The central final paths obscured the destination.** Removed both long bright paths through the lake. The upper perimeter stays connected from the lift past the inactive entrance to the puzzles. An isolated Heart landing at (0, -20, height 5) has a visible lava gap wider than the heroes' jump reach. After the four locks and existing response delay, one short basalt bridge joins the perimeter directly to the landing. The goal text directs players back to the golden Heart. Deliberate placement and both ending choices remain intact.

The fixes were preceded by failing regressions demonstrating the gate shortcut, wrapped-angle mismatch, non-solid pillars, checkpoint award during a fall, the no-plate walking bypass and anti-crush activation outside the gate footprint. Existing puzzle and recovery tests remained in place.
